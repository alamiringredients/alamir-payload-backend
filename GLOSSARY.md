# Website content

## Language

**Public content**:
The published website content available to anonymous visitors, excluding drafts and private form recipients.

**Section**:
An editor-controlled block of page or record content that can be reordered, duplicated, hidden or removed.

**Owner form**:
A form belonging to a particular website page, product, job or event. Its content can differ from the default used for future records.

**Form purpose**:
The visitor task a form supports: home enquiry, contact, quote, application, reservation, search, or a custom page form.

**Meeting booking**:
A visitor's reservation of an available meeting time at an event.
